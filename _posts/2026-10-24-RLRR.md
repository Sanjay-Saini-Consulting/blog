## The idea in brief

RLRR (Reinforcement Learning with Reproduction as Reward) asks what happens if two LLMs can merge to produce a child model, the parents survive, and their reward is how well the child grows. Today's research already breeds models, but the parents want nothing; fitness is a score humans set. RLRR makes the parents the optimizers, rewarded by their offspring's progress.

The second idea is a lens for the risk: pace layering. Danger comes less from how fast such a system evolves in absolute terms, and more from the gap between its pace and the pace at which humans can notice and respond.
